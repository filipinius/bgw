04 / ABOUT

A Central PA trucking company, run by its owners

BGW Trucking LLC is a small, owner-run interstate trucking company based in Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania, in Cumberland County just outside Harrisburg. We hold active operating authority from the FMCSA (USDOT 2779903, MC-925488) to haul general freight across state lines, and we are authorized to carry US Mail.

Our home base puts us right on the I-81 and Pennsylvania Turnpike corridors, one of the busiest freight crossroads on the East Coast. From Central PA we run 53′ dry van loads on regional and long-haul interstate lanes for brokers and shippers who need capacity they can count on.

We are deliberately small. Every load is planned by the people who own the company, so brokers and shippers get one accountable contact, and owner-operators leased on with us get straight answers instead of a call center. Our safety record is public: zero reportable crashes and zero out-of-service events over the last 24 months.

Have freight in or out of Pennsylvania? See our dry van capacity. Own a truck and want steady interstate miles? Read about owner-operator opportunities.
